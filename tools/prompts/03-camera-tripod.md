## Hunyuan3D notes

- **The tripod legs are the hardest part** — they're thin geometry. If Hunyuan3D struggles, try regenerating the image with slightly thicker legs (still slender, but with more visual weight).
- **Lens should be a separate chunky shape**, not flush with the body — Hunyuan3D will read this better if there's clear depth between body and lens in the source image.

## What "good" looks like

- Reads instantly as "camera on tripod" from a distance (drone flyby view)
- Tripod legs are stable-looking, not toothpicks
- Lens points horizontally (toward an imaginary view)
- Charming, not technical-spec realistic
